The problem

In 2012, burning seemed to jump overnight.

It didn't. The new VIIRS sensor sees small fires the older MODIS misses. MODIS is now retiring: without a way to compare the two, the first decade of fire history is lost.

Proof

Tested on years it had never seen.

Each year since 2012 was hidden, then rebuilt from MODIS alone and checked against what VIIRS actually saw.

The tool

One screen: what's burning, is it unusual, what's next.

  • This week's fires on the map, with where the wind carries their smoke
  • Unusual areas flagged against the same weeks in every earlier year
  • A plain verdict for any searched or clicked area: this month vs the 25-year median
  • Wind, humidity and a 5-day fire-spread outlook
  • Downloads: the original NASA detections and the calendar, for any dates

Impact

One honest fire record, from MODIS to VIIRS and beyond.

Who it helps

  • Early-warning and emergency analysts watching an area
  • Land managers planning prescribed burns
  • Scientists studying 25-year trends

Why now

MODIS is near the end of its life, and Suomi NPP VIIRS stopped on 2 November 2026. Without harmonization the fire record keeps splitting.

What's next

  • Any region in the world: the method only needs FIRMS
  • Hourly refresh of new detections
  • Cloud-aware coverage from daily fire masks
  • Alerts when an area turns unusual
