The problem

Old and new satellite records don't match.

more burning reported by VIIRS (since 2012) than by MODIS (since 2000), in the same area and the same years. MODIS is retiring. Without a correction, records before 2012 cannot be compared with today.

The tool

One screen: current fires, normal or not, what's next.

  • Current fires: every detection from the last 7 days
  • Normal or not: this month against the 25-year median, for any searched or clicked area
  • Unusual areas marked on the map
  • 25-year replay, month by month
  • Wind, humidity and a 5-day fire-spread outlook
  • Downloads: NASA detections and the calendar, any dates

Impact

Who needs a consistent fire record.

Users

  • Emergency management: tell a normal burn week from a bad one
  • Forestry and agriculture: plan and track burn seasons
  • Utilities, insurers, air-quality agencies: 25 years of comparable history

Why now

MODIS is near the end of its life, and Suomi NPP VIIRS stopped on 2 November 2026. Without a correction, each sensor change breaks the record again.

Next
